# Questionnaire (Google Forms, anonymous)
**Engagement (1-5 agreement):** (1) I follow one particular team regularly. (2) I would be disappointed if I missed my favourite team's match. (3) I follow football news and results even on days without matches.
**Volume:** How many football matches did you watch in the last 7 days? (0-4, or "5 or more")
**Per match, in order watched (up to 5):** enjoyment (1-10); importance (1-5); was your favourite team playing (Yes/No); did you watch another match? (Yes/No)
Timestamps are removed from the public dataset.
